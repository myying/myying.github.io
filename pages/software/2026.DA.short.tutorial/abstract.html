A series of small interactive widgets that demonstrate the core ideas of data assimilation — web ports of sections of the NEDAS tutorial notebooks, structured after Evensen, Vossepoel & van Leeuwen, Data Assimilation Fundamentals (Springer, 2022).

Tutorials outline — seven chapters across three parts, one interactive widget per chapter.

Ch 1 — Bayes' rule for Gaussians: two widgets — slide the prior and observation likelihood of a 1-D Gaussian, then observe one of two correlated variables and watch the joint contours and marginals of the 2-D posterior.

Ch 2 — Observation–State Correlation Explorer: drag a marker across a 2-D temperature field and watch the ensemble correlation map and the bivariate scatter respond, revealing how a single observation informs a local neighbourhood of the state — and why position uncertainty produces strongly negative correlations.

Ch 3 — The ensemble Kalman filter: the analysis as a precision-weighted average — scalar to vector — then as a linear combination of ensemble perturbations, updating a background field toward an observation live in the browser.
