Nonlinear filters

When the Gaussian assumptions fail, Bayes' rule is solved directly on a set of weighted samples — particle filters. A planned widget will show weight collapse: the particles pile onto a single trajectory as the filter struggles with a strongly nonlinear observation, and why resampling fights it.

→ Evensen et al., Data Assimilation Fundamentals (2022), Ch 9 — nonlinear filters.
