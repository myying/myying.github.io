Data Assimilation Interactive Tutorials

Tutorials outline

A short series of tutorials on the core ideas of data assimilation, taught through small interactive widgets — web ports of sections of the NEDAS tutorial notebooks. One concept per chapter, one widget per concept: drag, click, and watch the analysis respond.

All 7 chapters live.

  • 01
    Bayes' rule for Gaussians
    Combine a forecast with an observation to get the best estimate — first for one variable, then two.
  • 02
    Observation–state correlation
    One observation informs a whole neighbourhood: correlation maps and ensemble scatter from a 2-D field.
  • 03
    The ensemble Kalman filter
    The same update, done with an ensemble instead of a formula — including repeated cycles over time.
  • 04
    Localization and inflation
    Two fixes that make ensemble DA work with a small ensemble: localizing the covariance, and inflating the spread.
  • 05
    Challenges from dynamical systems
    Chaos and non-Gaussian errors, from a toy chaotic model and a simulated hurricane.
  • 06
    Nonlinear filters
    Particle filters, and when they beat the EnKF.
  • 07
    Parameter estimation
    Same storm-tracking widget as Ch 4, now guessing its own velocity as it cycles.

Suggested reading: Evensen, Vossepoel & van Leeuwen, Data Assimilation Fundamentals (Springer, 2022) — the book this series follows.
