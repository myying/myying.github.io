Uncertainties in a model simulation of a tropical cyclone

The NEDAS vort3d toy model simulates a tropical cyclone on a 2560 km × 2560 km f-plane domain (128 × 128 grid, 20 km resolution, 7 atmospheric levels + boundary layer; Zhu, Smith & Ulrich 2001, "a minimal three-dimensional tropical cyclone model"). Starting from 20 slightly perturbed initial states (drawn from a 200-member free-running pool), the 11-day forecasts spread out: tracks diverge, and some members intensify, weaken or decay while others stay coherent — the classic picture of forecast uncertainty.
