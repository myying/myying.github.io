Observation–State Correlation Explorer

A single temperature observation (red circle) sits on a 2-D field. The ensemble tells us how that observation correlates with every other grid point — the quantity ensemble DA runs on. Here, each member is the same warm blob with its centre nudged to a different spot, so its 4 K contour traces out a ring of coloured circles.

Try it: click a dot in the scatter to highlight that member, or click/drag on a map to move the black state marker — the correlation map and scatter update live.

Reading the panels

Left — the true field, plus every member's 4 K contour. Since only the blob's position varies between members, the contours form a band of displaced rings. Click a dot in the scatter to highlight one member; drag the black square to move the state marker.

Middle — the correlation between the observation and every grid point, estimated from the ensemble. It's a dipole: strongly positive right at the observation, negative on the far side of the blob — position uncertainty naturally produces that negative lobe.

Right — the same information as a scatter: each dot is one member's observed value (x) vs. its value at the marker (y). The slope of that cloud is the correlation. Move the marker across the dipole and watch the slope flip sign.
