Localization and inflation

Two knobs make the EnKF work in high dimensions: localization zeros out spurious long-range correlations born of sampling noise, and inflation widens the ensemble to compensate for its small size. A planned widget will add both as sliders on the correlation map — watch the scatter re-fill as inflation is dialed up.

→ Evensen et al., Data Assimilation Fundamentals (2022), Ch 10 — localization and inflation.
