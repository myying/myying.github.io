The ensemble Kalman filter

1 · A single analysis step

Chapter 1's weighted average, done for a whole field: each ensemble member is nudged toward the observation by the same gain, xa = xb + K(y − Hxb). This widget applies that update, live, to Chapter 2's exact ensemble and observation — the correlation map you saw there is the update pattern K.

Try it: drag obs error — small σo trusts the observation and pulls the ensemble tight onto the truth; large σo barely moves it. Step through members with the slider, or click/drag a map to move the marker.

Reading the panels

Background — the forecast ensemble, loose around the truth ring. Analysis — every member after the update, pulled toward the observation; the band tightens more the smaller σo is. Scatter — background points (aqua) moving to analysis points (red); the arrow shows one member's move.

2 · Cycling the filter

Real data assimilation loops: forecast, observe, analyze, repeat. Here a storm drifts at a constant, perfectly known speed past a 3×3 grid of ground stations, and each cycle applies the same update as above to the storm's centre.

Try it: hit run and watch the ensemble spread (blue) shrink every cycle while the error (red) stays near zero — with a perfect model, the mean is always right even as spread collapses. Chapter 4 changes one thing about this setup, and that stops being true.
