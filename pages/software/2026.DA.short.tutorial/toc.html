Data Assimilation Interactive Tutorials

Course outline

A short course on the core ideas of data assimilation, taught through small interactive widgets — web ports of sections of the NEDAS tutorial notebooks. One concept per chapter, one widget per concept: drag, click, and watch the analysis respond. The arc follows Evensen, Vossepoel & van Leeuwen, Data Assimilation Fundamentals (Springer, 2022): Bayesian foundations first, then the analysis schemes built on them, then what lies beyond linear-Gaussian.

4 of 8 chapters live — the rest are planned.
