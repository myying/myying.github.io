From best linear unbiased estimate to the Kalman filter

The analysis as a precision-weighted average — Chapter 1's Bayes result written as a matrix update, xa = xb + K(y − Hxb). A planned widget will animate the Kalman gain K as forecast and observation uncertainties change: more observation precision means a larger gain, and the analysis moves closer to the data.
